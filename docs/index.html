Wiring Drag from the round port on the right of a card towards the next card — the wire magnetises to the nearest port, and dropping it anywhere on a card uses that card's first free input. On touch: tap the output port, then tap the destination card or port.

Keeping it readable Arrange lays the blocks out in flow order, Compact shrinks every card to a mini overview (or the − button shrinks one card), and the target button on a card opens just that block with everything it is built from.
